About Prestige Inspections USA

Providing Thorough & Reliable Home Inspections

As a licensed, insured and experienced home inspector in New York, I provide thorough and reliable home inspection services with professionalism and attention to detail. My goal is to give every client clear, accurate information about the property they're buying or selling.

I am licensed by New York State to perform residential home inspections in New York.

Before inspections, I worked in related hands-on construction and trade fields — which gave me practical, real-world experience that I bring to every inspection. That background allows me to identify issues that go beyond a checklist.

I help homeowners, buyers, and sellers make informed decisions about their properties by providing thorough evaluations and detailed reports delivered within 24 hours of the inspection.
